NOTES:
- Join: f.1 is the first page of the block; it opens mid-sentence ("istam tanta ueneratione habeamus", end of an Epiphany sermon), so the preceding text is not in this block. The last line runs on to f.1v l.01 ("celebretis / elemosinis uestris deuotis orationibus"): the join holds.
- 02, 20: "ornat" and "accendat" each carry the same superscript sign, a horizontal wavy stroke with a hook at its left end, above/after the t; it differs clearly from this scribe's 9-shaped -us sign (habeam9 l.01, iacem9 l.11, monem9 l.24). Read -ur: ornatur, accendatur.
- 04, 16: "xp" + three minims with a curl above (same form in both lines; in l.16 it must be "dominum christum") = christum. There is no separate "in" before "eternum" ("ētnum" with bar).
- 07: "cui" + a 9-shaped curl written above "lau", no insertion sign; placed before laudem ("ad cuius laudem"). A repaired hole lies between "lau" and "dē"; the nasal bar on e is visible, so laudem; nothing appears lost in the hole.
- 08: rubric in red capitals "IN PVRIFIC̄." with an abbreviation mark on C; expanded purificatione (guide note in the right margin, brown ink, "i purificat' / s. marie", supports it).
- 09: red initial Q; the following display letters "VA" and an uncial (round) M, then "uis": Quamuis.
- 11: "sub iacem9" written as two words.
- 15: q-sign. Seen: q with a horizontal stroke crossing the descender at the baseline and running on to the right; NO superscript. The same sign stands on f.2v l.06, f.3v l.08, l.24, f.4v l.04; this scribe writes quod as "qd" (f.1v l.09, f.3 l.02, f.3v l.09) and qui in full, so quod is excluded by his practice; read quia, but no full spelling of quia occurs on ff.1-4v, so the reading rests partly on sense: quia(?). Alternatives: quod (Pass 1; no d is written), quam.
- 17: first letter an uncial-d-like form with a tall ascender looped at the top, then p, then t with crossbar; a brownish "&"-like mark (different ink, possibly later) over/after the t. Read Propter by sense; letters not certain. Alternatives: none fitting the letters found.
- 19: "Marie" with the round uncial M (as the display M in l.09); a point after it.
- 22-23: "can- / candelis" dittography at the line break, kept.
- Pass 2's "uos" (l.08) is wrong: the image has "uob" with a superscript = uobis.
