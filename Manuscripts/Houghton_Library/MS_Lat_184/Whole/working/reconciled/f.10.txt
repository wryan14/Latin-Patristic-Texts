NOTES:
01: new piece: red enlarged initial F (two lines high); red rubric "alius." with point after "nouissima"; brown guide note "alius." in the right margin. "Frs kmi" = Fratres karissimi.
05, 13: short horizontal dash in the right margin, not text.
09: word by word: "surgit·" | "Uidem(us)" (capital U, -us sign) | "romanū" = romanum | "imp(er)iū" — p with a horizontal stroke across the foot of the descender (the per-bar as on f.9) + "iu" + bar = imperium | "destrui" followed by a point | "De" — round-backed d enlarged above x-height (capital) | space | "in" with a superscript stroke = inde (same sign as "in̄ exiret" = inde exiret, 13). Reconciled: "Uidemus romanum imperium destrui. De inde et leges humane penitus defecerunt." De inde = deinde; written as two units with a space, as the scribe also does with "Qua cumque" (f.9v 13).
13: "nōne" written as one word = nonne; "cicius" (c-i-c-i-u-s, no t).
17: an "i" written above the final e of "benedicte" (correction to benedicti), no expunction point under the e. "pat" + superscript i + s = patris; "m·" = mei.
19: "dn̄m" — d + n + m (five minims) with bar = dominum (contrast "dm̄" = deum on f.9v 19, 21).
20: "sacertoti" with t for d, clear.
20-21: "si uult futurum iudicium dei" — no infinitive written; no insertion mark.
22: "Adtendite" (A, d, t).
24: point after "floret". Last line; descenders drawn long.
Join: f.9v ended a piece with its doxology; f.10 opens a new piece with rubric and initial — no running text across the page break, no break in sequence.
