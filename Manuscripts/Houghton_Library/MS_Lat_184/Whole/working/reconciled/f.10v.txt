NOTES:
01: red vertical stroke at the right edge beside "ieiuniis" (lines 01–02) is offset from the facing page, not text.
02, 15: q-sign: q whose stem rises a little above x-height, with a 2-/r-rotunda shape at the top right running into a long horizontal stroke across the descender; no superscript. Identical to f.9 03, 09. Line 14 has "quia" written in full in the same chain of clauses ("quia totum spatium … quia post mortem"). Read quia.
03: "sepat" with per-bar: separat.
04: "ult" + superscript open a (two-pronged flag) = ultra; ulterius would need -ius. Not fully certain of the superscript's value, but it is not the -er/-ur wave.
05: "re" + q whose stem rises above x-height with a small flag at the top and descends well below the baseline (so q, not d) + "em": requiem (q with superscript mark = qui). Point after.
10: closing formula written "q· sine f· uiuit ⁊ regnat·" ("f·" = fine; uiuit et regnat in full, the model for the expansion on f.9v 24). Red rubric "Alius." at the end of the line; brown guide note "alius" in the left margin. 11: new piece, red enlarged initial F; "Frs kmi" = Fratres karissimi.
21: no point after "repromissionis"; "rep" with pro-curl at the foot.
23: "mat" + superscript = mater.
24: "qui uis" written with a space. "iturus" clear (i-t-u-r-u-s). Last word pair: the ł-shaped sign (stem with a loop/stroke at the top right, base turning right) — the same sign as f.9v 04 "emunt uel uendunt" — then "in": uel in. Not the scribe's capital E (f.9 09, 15 etc.), which has a separate t. Long decorative descenders into the lower margin.
Join: begins "Igitur uigilate", continuing f.10 24 "sic uita nostra deficit." — a new sentence after a full stop; sense continuous (exhortation), joins. Ends "uel in" mid-sentence, continued on f.11 ("alium locum").
