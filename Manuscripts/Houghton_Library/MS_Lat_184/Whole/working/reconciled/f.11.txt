NOTES:
05: closing formula written "op(er)ib;· Qd u· p· d· qⁱ cū p· s· s· u· ⁊ r·". Expanded only where the letters or the scribe's own fuller wording support it: Qd = Quod, qⁱ = qui, cū = cum, "u· ⁊ r·" = uiuit et regnat (written out f.10v 10). "u. p. d." and "p. s. s." are kept as letters: likely sense "uobis prestare dignetur … patre (et) spiritu sancto"; no "et" before s. s. is written. Red rubric "alius" (lower-case a, x-height) after the formula; brown guide note "alius." in the right margin. 06: new piece, red enlarged initial U/V (Uos), red smudge below it.
07: "9sololabit" + ur-sign: consololabitur (dittography), as written.
14: "s; tristicia u· u· ı́ g·" — single-letter suspensions of the quotation; ı́ = in (conventional); u. u. g. expanded from the sense (uestra uertetur … gaudium), hence (?) on each. Point after g.
16: "uitias": the third letter has a flat top stroke crossing the stem (t), unlike the curved-top c of "diuicias" 22 and "diuicie" 10; written as seen.
21, 23: q-sign = quia (q + 2-shaped stroke + long horizontal, as f.9 03, f.10v 02/15).
24: "dep̄dant" — the bar is over the bowl of p (pre), the descender is plain (no per-bar at the foot), then the ur-wave: depredantur. "nullā": nasal stroke running from the top of the second l over the a = nullam (f.11v 01 continues "rentiam"). Long dash in right margin after the line (line filler).
Join: begins "alium locum", continuing f.10v 24 "uel in" ("uel in alium locum") — joins. Ends "nullam reue-", continued f.11v 01 "rentiam".
