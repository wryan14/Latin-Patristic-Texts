NOTES:
01: "suū": long s + four minims (u + u) with a bar over the last two = suum (not sibi, which would need b / superscript i).
02: no point between "paciunt(ur)" and "sitim".
03, 16, 22: "dm̄" — d + three minims (m), no n: deum (contrast "dn̄m" = dominum, f.10 19).
05: "ut facilius eos ad se." — no verb written; no insertion mark.
06-07: red shape in the right margin beside "ad / eum" is offset, not text; "ad" is legible under it.
08: closing formula written "Qui uiuit ⁊ r· v· ſ· ſ·": "r·" expanded regnat from the scribe's full wording (f.10v 10 "uiuit ⁊ regnat"); "v. s. s." kept as letters — likely sense "in secula seculorum", but the first letter is a clear v, not i, so it is not expanded. Red rubric "Alius" (enlarged uncial A) with a small red point; brown guide note "alius." in the left margin. 09: new piece, red initial N (faded) in the margin, "ecesse" in spaced display letters.
10: q-sign = quia (as f.9 03).
14-15: "fide" has no nasal bar; "uerita-" / "tē" with a clear bar = ueritatem; the word is split across the line (Pass 1 put all of "ueritatem" on line 14).
19: q whose stem rises into a small closed loop with a hairline tail; no further letters. Read que (= quae) as on f.9 05; alternatives qua / qualis. Same sign f.9 05. Not quanta: "O quanta" (20) is written q + superscript + "nta".
22: "qⁱ" = qui (twice), "qⁱ ī-" = qui in-.
24: "q" + superscript = quam.
14-23: lighter, more reddish brown ink (same hand), not rubric.
Join: begins "rentiam", completing f.11 24 "nullam reue-" (reuerentiam) — joins. Ends "se" mid-sentence; continued f.12.
