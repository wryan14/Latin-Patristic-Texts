NOTES:
Foliation: the second digit is a rounded open form (3 or 0); "23" fits the sequence (f.10v = 20, f.11v = 22).
01: p before "peccatis" has a stroke curling left from the foot of the descender (pro-form, as f.9 03); "suis" clear (sense would want nostris; written as seen).
02: rubric in red after "luit.": "Sermo inebdomada q̊ dr̃ ꝯmi·" — Sermo | in (written joined to ebdomada) | ebdomada (in full) | q + superscript e = que | d + r + stroke = dicitur | ꝯ + m + one minim with a hooked abbreviation stroke above + point. The rubric form gives only "communi" + general abbreviation stroke; no s is written. Read communis(?) after the brown guide note in the right margin (three lines: "sermo i ebdo / mada q̊ dr̃ / ꝯmunis", where "ꝯmunis" is written with s) and the predicate syntax; alternative communi. Faint pinkish shape below the rubric = offset.

03-04: "ꝯsue-" / "sit" as written (consueuit expected).
