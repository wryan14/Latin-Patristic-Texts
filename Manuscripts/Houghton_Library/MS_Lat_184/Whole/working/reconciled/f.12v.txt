NOTES:
{margin}: brown note "admonitio / ad sacerdotes" in the left margin beside 01–02 (guide for a rubric that was not executed). No later foliation on this side.
01: ends the piece begun f.12 03; "eide" with bar = eidem; "am̄" = amen.
02: new piece: red enlarged A + "POSTOL'" in spaced display letters. "plashemat" with p written above between s and h, + ur-sign: plas\p/hematur. Red stroke in the right margin by 02–04 = offset.
03: "Nos": the capital is the scribe's N (two uprights joined by a diagonal), identical to "Nom" (02) and "Nos" (24); his capital U is the b-shaped form ("Uere" 14).
05: "debem⁹" = debemus.
12: "spiritalib;" written in full; 23 "spūalib;" (sp + two minims + stroke + alib;) expanded to the scribe's own full spelling spiritalibus; alternative spiritualibus.
13, 24: "v" with a point before it (same form on both lines). Operator decision 2026-11-24 for consistency with f.13 ll.13-14 and f.16 l.20: bare "v." (distinct from the scribe's ł = uel) is printed uidelicet(?), alternative uel in its explanatory sense; English "that is" either way. 13: "mei" clear.
14: q-sign = quia (as f.9 03), then "qd" = quod.
19: "de" above the line with a comma-shaped caret below: \de/testamur.
20: q-sign: q + 2-shaped / r-rotunda stroke (the quia sign) with an added superscript hook ending in a dot above it — the same composite sign as f.9v 14. The superscript is not part of the plain quia sign, so the sign does not determine the word. One value read for both instances: quoque(?) (fits "Quod ipse quoque apostolus … dicit" and "quoque si multis criminibus cecidisset"); alternatives quia (base sign) / quamuis (f.9v only). "apls" = apostolus.
20-21: "pre-/dicatio" as written.
Join: begins "eidem domino nostro committo …", continuing f.12 24 "Illi(?)" — sense joins (the last word of f.12 itself unresolved). Ends "Nos gloriam" mid-sentence; continued on f.13.
