JOIN: f.15 l.24 "Ideo congruum uidebatur referre" + f.15v l.01 "uobis de dignitate ..." — joins. f.15v l.24 "nostra ult~" + f.16 l.01 "esse merita" — joins (nostra ultra esse merita).
00: pencil page number "27" (f.15 recto also reads 27), as seen.
02: t with superscript er-hook + "rore" = terrore; no i-m for timore.
03: "bonis bonis" written twice (dittography, no deletion marks).
04: "insidare": after the long s a single minim, then d (sic, for insudare). Rubric in red with point "Quando uolueris."; guide note, left margin, brown: "q̄ndo uolu- / eris". Red initial D on l.05.
05: a point after "kmi" at mid-height (karissimi.), touching the i's finishing stroke; low confidence.
07: "dn̄s": d + n + long s with stroke = dominus (not ds).
08: "n̄ solū": n with the general abbreviation curl = non (as "n̄ pot" l.11), not nisi.
11: "boni" without abbreviation mark (not bonum). "q\i/d" with separate superscript i = quid (cf. f.13 l.02).
14: "sic" with superscript curl = sicut.
15: the word after "faciat." is the capital E of this hand (tall stem with flag, as "Et" on f.14v l.12 and f.13v l.20) + t, followed by the plain quia sign (q + crossing stroke, no superscript): Et quia. Pass 2's "Rex(?)" misreads the E-form as R and the q-sign as x.
16: no point after "differt".
18: "q" with superscript i = qui.
19: point after "uictus". "uel" written out in full (u-e-l): the scribe's full form; cf. ł on f.13 l.22 and the bare v of f.13 ll.13–14 / f.16 l.20.
19–20: slit/hole with stitch holes in the left margin area; both lines begin to the right of it; letters seen inside the hole belong to the leaf beneath. No text lost.
24: "ult" with long t-crossbar at line end = ultra(?) (confirmed by sense of f.16 l.01 "esse merita"); page ends mid-sentence.
