NOTES:
JOIN (in): f.16 l.24 "et discal-" + f.16v l.01 "ciatis pedibus" — joins (discalciatis).
JOIN (out) — BREAKS: f.16v l.24 ends mid-sentence "... ab illo fugit deus. Et q\i/da~", but f.17 l.01 begins a new piece with a large red initial: "[F]ilii hominum usque quo graui corde. ut quid diligitis ..." (Ps 4:3). The dedication sermon (a second copy of f.13v ll.05–24, where the sentence continues "Et \postquam has/ sordes expurgetis. ornate templum ...") does not continue on f.17: text lost (missing leaf/leaves) or the copy was abandoned. Not resolvable from the images.
01: "ciatis" — c + i + a (not "ceatis"). Gap in "pe dibus" from a crease at the top of the leaf; no loss. "incedatis": "tis" underlined (deletion) with superscript "m⁹" above = incedamus.
02–05: the p-signs before peccatis, nostris, tocius, omnibus, domino, rege, clero end in a hook curling back to the left at the foot of the descender (the pro-form), unlike the per-p of f.16 l.01/02 whose foot is crossed by a straight stroke: pro throughout. Point after "peccatis".
03: "tocius" with c. Point after "necessitatib;" (separate from the -us sign).
04: "dn̄o" = domino (the scribe's normal sign, cf. f.13 l.12 "a dn̄o"); domno (for the pope) equally possible with this abbreviation.
08: after "uenire." in lighter brown ink, at the gutter edge: Q with a superscript stroke (= Qui, cf. "Qⁱa" f.13 l.21) + v + two minims, the rest running into the gutter fold. Most probably the closing cue "Qui uiuit (et regnat ...)" ending the rogation sermon before the new rubric; only "Qui u u" is visible, and a third minim (uiu-) may be lost in the gutter. Lighter ink: possibly added afterwards, same or near-contemporary hand. Not settled.
09: point after "kmi" (karissimi.) clearly visible. Large red initial F. Rubric in red, spaced letters: "In dedicat~" with abbreviation stroke and point = In dedicatione(?). Guide note, left margin, brown: "i dedicat".
11: small blank/erased space between Tironian et and "ornate".
12: "q\a/uis" with stroke through q = quamuis.
15: stained blank (erasure?) between "sacrificium" and "offeratur"; "ei" written above it.
19: "unu i" = unum in (no "omnes", unlike f.13v l.12).
23: "Cui⁹cūq;" = Cuiuscumque (9-sign present).
24: last word: q with a small vertical superscript stroke (the i-mark of quid/qui), d with a hooked stroke on its ascender, and a — letters q-i-d-a + abbreviation: quidam(?). Neither n nor o is written, so "quando" (both passes) is not supported by the letters, although the f.13v parallel (postquam) would suit a temporal conjunction. Unresolved.
