NOTES:
- Join with f.1: f.1 l.24 "ut diem istum celebretis" + f.1v l.01 "elemosinis uestris deuotis orationibus" — joins (one sentence). Pencil foliation "2" top left, not text.
- 02: "opib;" — the ";" is the -bus sign, no point after operibus. Closing formula written "Qd i. p̃." (Q + d with stroke, "i." with point, p with a superscript, point). Kept as written: the scribe never writes this formula out in full on ff.1-4v (f.4 l.22 and f.4v l.24 have "Qd ipse pst̄", still abbreviated). Likely sense "Quod ipse prestet/prestare dignetur".
- 03: red initial F, "frs kmi" with bars = Fratres karissimi; rubric "In .xl." in red at the right of the line; guide note in left margin "i q̄drag." (in quadragesima), brown ink.
- 05: "diuina" in the left margin (level with l.05) with two dots above, matching two dots above "dispositio": inserted before dispositio, same brown ink, probably same hand. A point after "puidit" (prouidit) is clearly written; no point after "specialit̃".
- 06, 10: "xl·" with a point after only (no point before).
- 06: "quid quid" written as two words. 07: repaired hole after "delinqueretis", no text lost (normal spacing either side).
- 14: "salti" with bar — saltim (alt. saltem).
- 21: "x" with a superscript c = christus (nominative); the full form "xpc" stands in l.08.
- 23: "p· t· ⁊c̄·" — suspended quotation kept as "p. t." (panem tuum); "⁊c̄" = et cetera.
- No q-sign (q + stroke) on this page; "qd" (l.09) = quod.
