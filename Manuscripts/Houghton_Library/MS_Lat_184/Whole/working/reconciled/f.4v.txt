NOTES:
- Join with f.4 HOLDS: f.4 l.24 "toto corde in- / uocate eam". Pencil foliation "8" top left, not text. The Assumption sermon ends on l.24 with the closing formula; the join to f.5 is outside this batch.
- 01: "intc̄edat" = intercedat; "p" with loop = pro.
- 02: "ꝑ quā" — per quam, with a nasal bar over the a (so quam is written q-u-a + bar, not with the q-stroke sign).
- 03: a bare "v" between "maris·" and "illuminatio", no abbreviation mark: uel(?) (alternatives ut, et).
- 04: q-sign. Seen: q with a horizontal stroke through the descender, NO superscript (as f.1 l.15, f.2v l.06, f.3v l.08, l.24). Read quia(?): this scribe writes quod as "qd" and quam as "quā" (l.02), so neither is this sign; no full quia on ff.1-4v, so (?) kept.
- 06: "suasione" without abbreviation mark (as written).
- 10: "ꝑ qua" — NO nasal bar over the a (contrast l.02 "quā"); kept as written "per qua" (sense: per quam).
- 15: "ē aquā" — est + "aquā" written together: "a quam" (no d written; sense: ad quam).
- 17: "p" whose descender ends in a leftward hooked loop (as f.2v l.09) = pro; "q" with superscript e = que.
- 19: "recipiat" underlined (deletion by underlining), followed by "respiciat".
- 21: "Un̄" = Unde (same form as f.2 l.24). q-sign WITH the small superscript hook (as f.3 l.06, l.10, f.4 l.03, l.10): quoque(?) by sense; alternative quidem.
- 23: "deflete" as written; the stroke below its final e is the insertion caret belonging to l.24, not a mark on the word. A red smudge at the right edge of l.23-24 = offset/stain.
- 24: "penit" + the general abbreviation curl over t: the ending is not determined — penitendo(?) (alternatives penitentia, penitentie). "ura" with stroke (uestra) written above between "pecca" (peccata, bar) and "diluatis", with a comma-shaped caret below: \uestra/. Closing formula "Qd ipse p̃st̃" = Quod ipse prestet(?) (alternative prestare ...).
