NOTES:
- Join f.4v → f.5: BREAK in sense. f.4v ends with the closing formula "Quod ipse prestet(?)."; f.5 l.01 opens with a commendation/blessing formula "Modo committo corpus et animam uestram ..." (capital M, not an enlarged initial), ending "... ut participes fiatis eterne beatitudinis." before the rubric of the Nativity sermon. Either a free-standing formula appended after the Assumption sermon, or text lost between f.4v and f.5; the image of f.5 alone cannot decide.
- 03: "hic interris" written as one unit: three minims + t + e + r + r + one minim + long s. Read in terris (compare l.22 "inicium", where the i's foot likewise runs into the next minim and makes "in" look like "u"). Only one minim after "rr" excludes Pass 1's "uiterius". The hook below the line under "pmereamini" is the pro-loop of p (loop from the descender to the left), not an interlinear "ci".
- 06: "matr" + e, with a small i (with a short crossing tick) written above the e: a correction matres → matris; recorded matr\i/es (the e is not cancelled). Two dots stand above-left of the long s of "state"; the left margin has the same two dots before "sic" (main-hand ink as far as can be seen): insertion "sic state". "state" itself is clear (long s, t, a, t, e).
- 11, 12: q-sign: q with a short straight horizontal stroke through the descender, no superscript. Expanded quia(?) (both times). quia is never written out on ff. 5–8v; the same sign recurs at f.7v l.04, 22, f.8 l.02, f.8v l.07, always where only quia fits, and the scribe's other q-forms are taken (qđ = quod, q + superscript i = qui, q + superscript open a = quam/qua, q + 2-shaped stroke + superscript = quoque(?), f.7 l.15). Distinct from "qđ" (q + d with a curl on the ascender) = quod, l.02.
- 14: "Adtendite": capital A (uncial form) + round d with a short ascender + tendite. Not "Att-".
- 22: "inicium" twice: the letter after "ini" is c (no stem above the line, unlike t in "eterni" beside it).
- q-sign form A (q + straight stroke across the stem, no superscript) is written quia(?): quia is never spelled out on ff. 5–8v, so the default is not confirmed by the scribe; quia is the only one of his q-words not otherwise assigned (qđ = quod, q + sup. i = qui, q + sup. open a = quam/qua, q + 2-shaped stroke + sup. hook = quoque(?)). Same treatment as the f.4/f.4v reconciliation.
