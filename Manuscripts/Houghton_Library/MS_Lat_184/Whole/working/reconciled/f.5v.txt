- Join f.5 → f.5v: "Boni / uero exultant" — joins.
- 09: "de ė" written as two words (deest). Closing formula "Qđ ipse p̃st." : p with a curl above (pre) + s + t + point; no ending is written. prestet(?) kept; the scribe nowhere on ff. 5–8v writes the formula out in full (alternative: prestare dignetur or similar).

- 14: p with a loop from the descender to the left = pro (pro malo).

- 23: "9piscim9": con-sign + p (no bar or loop) + iscimus; the syllable cu is not written. concupiscimus(?) as the intended word; literally "con-piscimus".
- 24: "alii": two minims with two oblique i-strokes above (as on "iudicii" f.5 l.12), no nasal bar → alii, not alium.
