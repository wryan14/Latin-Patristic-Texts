NOTES:
- Join f.5v → f.6: "tunc diabolus / cum angelis suis preualet" — joins.
- 01: point after "dr" (dicitur) before Michahel. 10: point after "istud".
- 10: "michahel" with a hook-stroke after the l (absent from nominative "Michahel" in l.01) → Michahelis(?); uninflected Michahel possible.
- 12: "ininuicē": 9 minims before c (counted on the image: i+n | i+n+u+i) → in inuicem, not inuicem. "scōs" above "fugare"/"an-" with a hook below the line after "fugare,": insertion \sanctos/ (main hand).
- 13: "an-/glos": the l carries the same oblique stroke up from the ascender as "anglis" (l.01), which both passes expand angelis → an-gelos.
- 15: two dots stand above the a of "ab" (after "uestras"); the right margin has the matching dots before "cū ex.." : first word cū = cum; second word a tall x-like letter with a small superscript, then e/a-shape, r with a superscript stroke — read exierint(?), letters only partly certain (exierit possible).
- 16: closing formula "Qđ ipse pst." as f.5v l.09 → prestet(?). Rubric red "In nat͛ omnium scōꝝ": "nat" + superscript sign — natale(?); natali / natiuitate equally possible from the sign. Guide note in right margin brown "omniū scōꝝ".
- 18: after "pro" the parchment is torn and repaired; a dark trace (like an a) shows inside the repaired hole, probably seen through the repair, not read. "pro-/mittit" is continuous, so no text appears to be lost.
- 19: "regnū": after g four minims (n + u) with the nasal curl above → regnum (not regni).
- 22: "certu" — two minims after t (u, not i); a small stroke stands over r/t rather than over the u → certum(?), literally "certu". "ppi": first p with pro-loop, second p with a superscript i → proprii.
- 23: q-sign: q with a stroke through the descender and a superscript open a (u-shaped curl with upturned tips). The identical sign appears in "numq^a" (l.07 numquam) and "aliq^a" (l.08 aliquam) → quam. Distinct from q + stroke without superscript (quia, f.5 l.11–12).
- 24: "frs" above, with a hook below the line after "Uidetis": \fratres/. No point after it.
