NOTES:
- Join f.7 → f.7v: "Unde pariter / oremus hodie" — joins.
- 01: "int" + superscript hook + "uentu" = interuentu (not intuentu).
- 02: q-sign of the SECOND form (q with a short 2-/3-shaped stroke from the top right of the bowl, ending in a blob, + superscript hook) — the same sign as f.7 l.15, 24 → quoque(?) ("Monemus quoque uos ... ut"); quia is excluded by the shape (contrast l.04, l.22).
- 04, 22: q-sign of the FIRST form: q with a long straight horizontal stroke crossing the stem at the baseline, no superscript → quia(?) (as f.5 l.11–12).
- 06: "Opem" + superscript ur-sign (no er-sign on the p) → Operemur(?); "ǵ" = ergo.
- 07: "fide" with nasal curl = fidem; Tironian et (clear).
- 10: point after "pauperes"; "Demus" begins with a capital (uncial D).
- 11: "potum" above the second "cibum" (same ink), no deletion mark on "cibum": kept.
- 18: "Qđ ipse p" — p with a short flag at the top right; the rest of the formula is not visible: prestet(?) as on ff. 5v–7. Then faint grey traces of two or three letters (erased, illegible) before the rubric.
- 18: rubric (red, faded): "In" + display q (bowl with a tail, no long descender) + u + o + a pale/thin tall l-like stroke + e + u with superscript stroke + q; + "festo." Literally "In quo l e ū q; festo". The guide note in the left margin (brown, beside l.19) is clear: "ı̄ qͦcūq; festo" = in quocumque festo. The rubric is read as a (miscopied?) In quocumque(?) festo; "le" stands where "c" is expected. Not "apostoli cuiusque" (letter count and forms do not fit).
- 19: red initial C (two lines); "ONSULI" in spaced black display capitals, then a bowl with a tail descending to the left (display M?) with superscript 9 (-us): CONSULImus(?) — Consulimus; the M is the uncertain letter.
- q-sign form A (q + straight stroke across the stem, no superscript) is written quia(?): quia is never spelled out on ff. 5–8v, so the default is not confirmed by the scribe; quia is the only one of his q-words not otherwise assigned (qđ = quod, q + sup. i = qui, q + sup. open a = quam/qua, q + 2-shaped stroke + sup. hook = quoque(?)). Same treatment as the f.4/f.4v reconciliation.
