NOTES:
- Join f.7v → f.8: "coram oculis / uestris" — joins.
- 02: q-sign form A (q + long horizontal stroke across the stem at the baseline, no superscript) → quia(?).
- 04: "diuitiis": after "diui" a t (straight top bar running from a stem on the left), not the curved c of "incorde" l.22; two oblique i-strokes over the last ii in lighter brown ink (possibly added later).
- 06: "pro" written above, with a hook below the line between "n̄" and "sunt": non \pro/sunt.
- 10–11: "offendim9" (offendimus) stands alone in the right margin at the height of l.10, main-hand ink. NO insertion sign was found: none beside the marginal word, none in l.10 after "maximam", none in l.11 between "dm̄" and "tunc" (checked at 3.5–4×). Placed after "deum" in l.11 because the sentence lacks its verb there ("Quando deum … tunc nusquam oportet nos esse securos"); the placement is by sense only, hence (?). Physically: {margin: offendimus} beside l.10.
- 11: "nusq" + superscript open a = nusquam (as written).
- 17: "int̃" at line end = inter (whole word).
- 19: q + superscript open a (two-pronged, as in numquam/aliquam f.6) = quam.
- 20: "relinqt" with a superscript open a over q/t — the same mark as on "qͣndo" just before it — = relinquat (not the 2-shaped ur-sign) → "cui relinquat. et quando relinquat".
- 22: "radicent." — t with its bar drawn out to the right, no ur-sign → radicent (not radicentur).
- 24: "grē scī spē." — sp + e(c?) with a bar: spiritus(?) (form unusual; case ending not shown).
- q-sign form A (q + straight stroke across the stem, no superscript) is written quia(?): quia is never spelled out on ff. 5–8v, so the default is not confirmed by the scribe; quia is the only one of his q-words not otherwise assigned (qđ = quod, q + sup. i = qui, q + sup. open a = quam/qua, q + 2-shaped stroke + sup. hook = quoque(?)). Same treatment as the f.4/f.4v reconciliation.
