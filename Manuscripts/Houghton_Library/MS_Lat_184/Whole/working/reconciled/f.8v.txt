NOTES:
- Join f.8 → f.8v: "cordi uestro bonam / uoluntatem" — joins. Last line runs on to f.9 ("si quis").
- 01: "Qđ ipse p." (p + point only) → prestet(?). Rubric red "Itē in qͦlib; f." = Item in quolibet f(esto); guide note left margin "itē iqͦlib;" / "festo." 
- 02: "nolum9": l + u + m (five minims) + us-sign → nolumus (not nolimus); "Uelim9" four minims → Uelimus.
- 04, 05, 06: "un̄" = u + n + the general abbreviation curl (the same curl as "n̄" = non in l.06 and "Un̄" = Unde l.14) → unde (Pass 1's ubi would be u + superscript i, as on f.6 l.22).
- 07: q-sign form A (q + horizontal stroke across the stem at the baseline) → quia(?). "n" + an upright superscript i (not the curl of "n̄" = non in l.06) → nisi.
- 08: point after "uestram", then capital I (tall) "Ipe" with superscript = Ipse.
- 11: "q" with superscript open a + descender stroke = quam; "aliq" + superscript o = aliquo.
- 13: "inp̄senta": p with bar above = pre; the letters after "sen" are t + a with no i visible → presentia(?) (literally "presenta"). A row of expunction dots runs under the whole of "lectione" and "di" (= dei) is written above it: [[lectione]] \dei/.
- 15: "nob" above with a hook below the line before "por-": insertion \nobis/.
- 17: rubric red "alius sermo."; guide note left margin "alius s:".
- 18: line ends with "g" + superscript open a (two-pronged): read gloria(?) — the sign itself (g + a) would more usually stand for gratia; gloria / gratia both open.
- 19: "sum" + superscript 9-shaped us-sign → sumus (not sunt).
- 21: point after "conuertatur".
- q-sign form A (q + straight stroke across the stem, no superscript) is written quia(?): quia is never spelled out on ff. 5–8v, so the default is not confirmed by the scribe; quia is the only one of his q-words not otherwise assigned (qđ = quod, q + sup. i = qui, q + sup. open a = quam/qua, q + 2-shaped stroke + sup. hook = quoque(?)). Same treatment as the f.4/f.4v reconciliation.
