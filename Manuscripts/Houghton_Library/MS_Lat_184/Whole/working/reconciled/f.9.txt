NOTES:
03: q-sign: q whose stem rises above x-height, with a 2-shaped / r-rotunda-like stroke attached to the right of the stem and a long hairline tail sweeping right; no superscript letter. Same sign at 09 and on f.10v 02/15, where f.10v 14 has "quia" written in full in the same construction; quod is always "qd" on this page (07, 11, 13, 20). Read quia throughout. Next: p with a loop curling left from the foot of the descender = pro (contrast the straight bar of per at 09, 15, 22); then "his" (h, i, long s).
05: q whose stem rises and closes in a small loop with a hairline tail to the upper right (no nasal bar, no further letters); read que (= quae); alternatives qua / qualia. Not quanta: the scribe writes quanta as q + "nta" (f.11v 20). Same sign f.11v 19 ("Uidete que sit uoluntas").
07: "m·" with point = mei (suspension point, as the points in 09).
09: "t̄ q h· m· e·" — t with abbreviation stroke (tibi), the quia sign of 03, then single-letter suspensions with points; h. m. e. expanded from the sense of the quoted words (hodie mecum eris), hence (?) on each.
09, 15, 22: p with a straight horizontal bar through the descender = per (not propter: no superscript t / second p). "per hoc" each time.
10: no point after "latronib;" (the ; is the -us sign); point after "peccatorib;".
15: two-dot insertion sign after "dedit"; "dns" (dominus) with the same sign in the left margin; same hand and ink.
23: capital Z: horizontal top bar running left of the stem, vertical stroke, foot to the right (not L, which has no top bar).
24: "de" above the line with a comma-shaped caret below the line after "multos". "q" with superscript i = qui.
Join: begins "fornicator", continuing f.8v 24 "Si quis adulter est. si quis" — joins. Ends "ex" mid-sentence; continues on f.9v.
