NOTES:
02: "per hoc" with straight-barred p; "promeruit" with p looped to the left at the foot (pro); same pro-form 09.
04: between "emunt" and "uendunt" a sign like ł (stem with a stroke crossing the top, base turning right), sitting on the line; not the scribe's tironian et, which is a 7-shape descending below the line (02, 08). Read uel.
06: superscript "a" with a caret below the line: inserted letter, thelone\a/rius.
13, 18: "Qua cumq;" and "qui cuq;" written with a clear space between the parts; kept as two words.
14: q-sign: q with the same 2-shaped / r-rotunda stroke attached to the stem as the quia sign (f.9 03, 09), plus an added superscript mark above it — a short stroke rising right into a dot. The superscript is not part of the plain quia sign, so the sign does not determine the word. Same composite sign f.12v 20 ("Quod ipse … apostolus"), where quia cannot stand; one value read for both: quoque(?); alternatives quia (base sign) / quamuis (sense "etiam si" here).
19, 21: "dm̄" — d + three minims (m) with the bar hooked from the d; no n (contrast "adnm" = ad dominum 14 and "dnm" 08). Read deum.
20: "apiet" with the wavy superscript ur-sign, the same mark as "dabit(ur)" in the same line: aperietur.
24: "Ad" with a capital A; point after "pducat". Closing formula written "q· sine fine· u· r·": expanded qui sine fine uiuit et regnat; "et" is not written (the scribe writes "uiuit et r." in full form on f.11v 08), hence (?). Long decorative descenders into the lower margin; small red speck below "fine" (not text). END of the piece; no rubric or explicit on the page.
