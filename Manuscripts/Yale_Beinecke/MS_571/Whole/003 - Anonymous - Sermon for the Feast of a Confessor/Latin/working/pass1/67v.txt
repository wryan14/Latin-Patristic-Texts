NOTES:
02: "neelemosina" written without word space; line end "qua" + gutter: quia (i not clearly seen) vs. quam/quod.
05: left margin lection mark in lighter brown ink, "Lc" + numeral, read viii(?) (alternatives: vii, uiii with suspension). A small horizontal dash/paragraph mark also stands in the left margin between lines 6 and 7.
06: "huius" (hui + us-sign) in small lighter later hand above "ill."; a small stain/hole below "post". Line end "ire" curves into gutter; could be "ire" or "ir-" with more hidden.
07: erasure of c. 5-6 letters after "et"; faint traces look like "ut h.c" / "ut hec" with a descender-mark (possibly "uc hꝫ e"); very uncertain.
08: main scribe "sustentat" with bar over -ta-(?); "n" and the 2-shaped -ur sign added above the line (dark ink, possibly main hand correcting) = sustentantur.
09: "possum'" read possumus (us-sign); possimus not excluded, the letter before m looks like u. "laudabilit" + sign at gutter.
10: after "obtinere." a smudged/partly erased short word on the line ("sus"? "fus"? "siu"?), over which a small later lighter hand wrote "poscam'" (first letter seems to carry a bar: "p̄oscam'"?; alternatives: possimus, poscamus). Then "eam'" with us-sign = eamus (or eam + mark).
15: 2-shaped -ur sign above "lucret" appears added in lighter ink; no point visible after lucretur. Line end "detrimentu" + bar lost in gutter.
16: -ur sign(s) after "patiat" doubled in lighter ink; line end "commutation" + gutter, ending (-em) not seen.
17: "pro" = p with loop to the left, standing out into the margin.
19: line end "abnegem" + gutter; -us sign not clearly seen.
20: "ea" in lighter ink in the left margin directly before "que" (later addition). Line end "au"/"aua" in gutter, continues "ritia" (written "rítia" with a stroke over i) on 21.
22: last word in gutter, "que" (or "quae"/"qui") doubtful.
23: line end "reg" + descending flourish in gutter; "-num" not seen (line 24 begins "dei"), contrast line 12/13 where "reg-/num" is divided.
24: "crucem" smudged at the gutter; possibly followed by further letters lost in binding.
