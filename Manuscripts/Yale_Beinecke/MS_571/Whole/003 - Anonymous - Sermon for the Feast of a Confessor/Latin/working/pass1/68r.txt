NOTES:
01: first word "xpi" with faint bar, in the dark mottled corner; "eum" written "eu" with a faint bar (could be read "eu" only). "hos-" at line end continues "tiam" in 02 (start of 02 is faint: "tiam" read under mottling).
03: line begins "m" with a small superscript 9-sign = "mus" (end of crucifigamus); "uetere" with bar = ueterem; "homine" with bar = hominem.
04: "inpuditiciis": letters after "inpudi" read t-i-c-i-i-s; c/t are hard to tell apart in this hand, alternatives inpudiciciis / inpudicitiis. No point seen between "cubilibus" (b;) and "et".
07: margin note in small capitals-like later script, outer margin level with this line: "Tu autem" (lection-ending cue); the last letters ("em") faint/greyish, partly under the repair strip. Enlarged initial A in "Ante".
08: "fatiem": third letter looks like t (flat top joined to i) rather than c; alternative "faciem". "humiliter" = "humilit" with bar.
09: a point stands after "innumeras" (kept as written).
10: "qui" = q with superscript i, standing just before the triangular hole; nothing seems lost in the hole (dark marks inside it belong to another leaf). Small 9-like mark above "referamus" = -us sign.
11: a small + above the line after "usque" answers "+ nunc" in the outer margin (lighter ink, thinner pen: later hand?). "gladiis" written "gladus"-like (ii without distinction); alternative "gladio" unlikely.
13: "peccantes" has an accent-like stroke over the e. "punit" with a small i above between n/i and t: read pun\i/it (= puniit); alternative "puniuit" not supported by the letters.
15: "propheta" – a bar over the final a is not clearly visible under the mottling; alternative "propheta." without bar. "Nolo" with enlarged N.
16: small hole inside "uiuat" (ui{hole}uat), a curved stroke under the hole is a scribal tie/bridge mark, no text lost. "Illum" = "Illu" with bar, enlarged I. "laudem" + superscript 9 = laudemus.
18: + after "est" answers a marginal "+ don…" in the outer margin after "populus", faint, with a stroke above the last letters: read "donum" (alternatives "dona", "donu"); lighter ink, probably the same later hand as in 11. "deprecemur" = "dep̄cem" with the -ur sign (2-shaped) above.
19: "pascue" sic (e for ae). "manuum eius" faint at the damaged edge.
20: gap after "per" (p with stroke) caused by the rough area above the hole of line 21; "sanctorum" = "scoꝝ" with bar.
21: "Nec" with enlarged N (dark blot on it). "per" (p with stroke) then hole, then "mittat": read per{hole}mittat, one word. The letter "n" and a stroke visible inside the hole belong to another leaf and are not transcribed.
22: "uitiorum" = "uitioꝝ"; c/t ambiguous, alternative "uiciorum". Marks inside the hole are from another leaf.
23: "uirtu" – hole – "tum": the letters before the hole are rubbed ("uirtu" probable), an "a"-like shape seen in the hole belongs to another leaf. After "frui" a point, then the parchment is lost (missing corner); whether anything followed on this line cannot be seen. The words "…edo" and "…dubio" visible beyond the torn edge belong to the next leaf and are not transcribed.
