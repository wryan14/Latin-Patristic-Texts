NOTES:
01: a small accent-like stroke stands above between "in" and "ea" (ínea); not an abbreviation, read "in ea". "transitorie" ends in e/e-caudata at the binding edge; final letter slightly compressed.
02: "curricula" is written with a gap after "cur" (parchment flaw), one word. "que" and "celis" with e-caudata.
03: "peruenire": p with bar through descender (per) + "uenire"; an -us sign stands above the end of "ualeamus" and of "Offeramus".
05-06: "Dili-/gamus": enlarged D at the line end in the gutter; "Dili" read with reasonable confidence.
06: "Qui": enlarged Q at the line end, "ui" small and curving into the gutter; could be read "Qui" only (no further letters seen).
10: between "suum;" and "Omnes" a triangular hole shows letters ("ue"/"re") of another leaf; not transcribed. "Omnes" = "Oms" with bar; "autem" = "aut" with bar.
11: "domino" = "dno" with bar at the gutter edge (dative); "dominus/domini" not excluded from the image alone but o seems clear.
12: "sacrificia": followed by punctuation mark; "de-" at the line end continuing "cimas".
14: "nostrorum" = "nror(um)" at the gutter; "labiorum" with -rum sign.
15: "cetera" with e-caudata on first e (written "cętera").
16: between "pigri." and "ad" a small round hole with a letter-like shape (resembling "et"/"r") from the leaf beneath; I take it as show-through in a hole, not an insertion sign; alternative: an "et" written by the scribe (then "pigri. et ad ista"). "fideliter" = "fidelit" with -er bar at the gutter.
17: "persoluenda": p with bar (per), a stroke above "ue" looks like an accent or stray mark (not a nasal bar, since "en" is written out). "quoniam" = "qm" with bar. "quod" = "qd" with stroke.
18: "etenim" written "& enim" (et + eni with bar).
19: "spiritum" = "spm" with bar.
20: "produxit": beginning of the line near the curved edge; "ce" + "produxit" clear. "illius" = "illi" + -us sign.
21-23: holes interrupt the lines; the scribe wrote around them (nequaquam {hole} possumus; bo{hole}nos; in{hole}iustos); letters visible inside the holes belong to another leaf.
22-24: at the left beyond the curved edge, writing of a neighbouring leaf ("pa...", "di non a...") is visible; not transcribed.
24: line begins indented because of the curved edge. "uias" could also be read "uias" only (minims u-i + as). Last word cut by the lower-right edge: "scir" clearly visible followed by a trace (e or point); "scire" (?) — continuation not visible.
