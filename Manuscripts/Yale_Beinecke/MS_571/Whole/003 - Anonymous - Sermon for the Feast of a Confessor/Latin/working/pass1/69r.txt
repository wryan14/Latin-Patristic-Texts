NOTES:
04: the scribe first wrote "sustentator"; a small u is written above the o (no clear expunction dot visible, the spot is stained) = correction to "sustentatur". The u looks like the main hand/ink, so not marked {later}.
05: between "Hunc" and "mag-" a comma-/7-shaped stroke hangs below the baseline, close to the m. I take it as punctuation or a pen flourish, not a word; alternative: an insertion/attention sign. Nothing corresponding seen in the margin.
06: "timeam9" and 06/07 "ame-/m9": -mus by 9-sign, clear.
07: "aeterni" is written with an a+e ligature (not e-caudata), hence ae kept. "ut nos" could be divided "utnos"; no doubt about letters.
11: "a celorum": only "a" is written (not "ad"); kept as on the page. "felitia": the letter after i looks like t (flat top) rather than c; a small stroke/accent stands above the e–l ("fél"); alternative reading "felicia". In the left margin beside this line a small round hole/stain shows two letters ("a" above, "l"/"i" below) which I take to be writing of another leaf, not transcribed.
14: "timetur": e written above the line between m and t, main hand. "dei": written di with bar; alternative "diuisio" rejected because of the bar and the word space.
15: large oval hole after "cernitur" and a second smaller damaged spot to its right; letters seen inside ("inf…", and minims above) belong to another leaf. No punctuation visible between "cernitur" and "intentissime". "deprecemur": p with bar above (pre) + 2-sign. "que" = q with e and bar (quae); alternative "quia" not supported.
16: "ac{hole}cuset": the scribe wrote "ac", skipped the hole, continued "cuset" (single s).
17: a tiny hole/defect between "no" and "s" of "nos", with a dark mark above it (possibly show-through or a dot); the word is "nos".
19: "ill." written "itt" with stroke through the two l's; nothing written above it that I can see.
20: after "obtentu" a punctus-elevatus-like mark (point with tick above), given as ";".
21: above "senti" (over the n–t) a small round stained spot, probably a tiny hole with dark traces inside; I do not read it as an addition. "auxiliis" written "auxilijs" with long final i.
22: "futura": "futa" with 2-shaped sign above (-ur-). "celestibus": e-caudata, -bus by b;.
23: "auxiliante" has a point-like mark after "auxili" (auxili:ante), apparently a parchment flaw/line-filler; "et" before "deo" is faint (paler ink).
24: "nostro" = nro with bar (read, not "pro"). Large hole after "qui in"; writing visible in the hole ("…us non…") and below/right of the flap ("…dubio") belongs to another leaf. "sanctis" = scis with bar; "semper" = semp with crossed p; "est" is written raised, above the line level, on the flap beside a repaired tear, followed by a point.
