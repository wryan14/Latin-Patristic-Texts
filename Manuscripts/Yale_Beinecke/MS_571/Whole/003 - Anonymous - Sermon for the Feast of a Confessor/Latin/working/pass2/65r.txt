NOTES:
01: the first half of this manuscript line ("celum diues ingreditur;") belongs to the preceding text and is not transcribed. Rubric NATA- divided at line end.
02: second rubric word reads V-N(or H)-I-V-S; taken as UNIUS (N with a high crossbar looks like H); alternative CUIUS unlikely (no C seen). Rubric ink faded.
03: KARISSIMI is abbreviated (KMI with bar); expansion by rule. A hole follows "celebrandam"; writing of another leaf ("non", "...bus") shows through it and is ignored. No text lost, line continues on 04.
04: "sacri" has a dot under the i and a small o above it: read as correction sacri -> sacro (sacrosancti). Hole after "confessoris"; nothing lost.
05: small pale word above/after "ill.": read "aderaldi" (first letter a or A; could also be "adesaldi"); later hand.
06: a small "a" written above "conuenitis" between n and i/t (above "ni"); placement uncertain — perhaps intended correction toward "conuenistis"/"conueniatis"; recorded as seen.
07: "actentissime" spelled with ct (= attentissime); "omnibus" has a blot over the o.
08: second "omnibus" written "omibus" with bar.
11: "mores" — the r appears doubled or retouched ("morres"?); read mores. "preteritorum" = p with bar above + -orum sign.
12: small superscript word between "uixisse" and "pudet", pale ink, read "eos" (alternatives "se", "eis"); hand uncertain, possibly later. "transeant": bar over a, plus a small tick above — nasal bar taken.
13: "lumen" abbreviated "lum" + bar.
17: "uestrum" abbreviated urm with bar.
19: blot in "nouitate" before the final e (nouita[t]e); "sensus" = sens + superscript us-sign (shaped like a 2/9; if 2 = -ur it would be meaningless, so -us). "probetis" = p with pro-loop.
20: run-over at lower right, preceded by a paragraph/run-over bracket sign: "dei bona".
