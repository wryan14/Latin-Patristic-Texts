NOTES:
01: "Hec est": written Hec + e with bar (= est). "uoluntas": final s faint, read from its long-s shape; no "dei" is visible after it. "ut be-" at the binding edge, legible.
02: "faciatis": middle letters smudged (faci..tis); faciatis fits the strokes.
03: margin mark very faded: L/lc + about three minims (iii); could be another number. Line end "consur" – last letters run into the gutter; "consur-/git" read with line 04 start "git". Large initial Q in Quidam.
06: "inmundorumque": -orum sign followed by q; "spirituum" = spuum with bar.
07: "ad tam": adta with bar over a; alternative "ad tantum" rejected (no second t visible). "et ira": possibly "et ita" – second letter of the word could be t; I read r.
08: text starts after the large hole with "ris" (continuing confesso-). "quod": q + d with bar and a small cross-shaped stroke above at the edge; alternative "quam"/"quid".
09: text starts after the second, smaller hole; nothing lost. "uiuentium": middle blurred (uiuen..um).
12: "ambulauit": minims crowded, could be read "ambulauit" only by count. "non" at the edge small.
13/14: "iudi-/tio": line 14 begins "tio" or "cio"; t and c indistinguishable here.
15: margin mark faded: lc. + four minims (iiii)?; enlarged initial N.
16: "ecclesie": written eccle with cross-stroke through l and e-caudata; written in the line (slightly larger spacing), not an addition.
17: after "clementiam" a thin oblique stroke (paragraph/insertion slash) before the capital "Haben-"; "Haben" – last letters in the gutter.
18: "per-": p with bar through descender at the line end.
21: "in" stands in the left margin in a browner ink before "illos" – taken as a later marginal addition belonging to "exardescit in illos"; could be by the main scribe.
22: "inuidiam": end in the gutter, reads inuidia + bar(?).
23: "hac" clearly written with h (= ac).
24: "omnia": oma with bar and a small "a" written above between omnia and prorsus – either correction of the ending or plain superscript a; above "iniquitatis", in brown ink with a bracket-like sign before it, an interlinear word of about five minims + long cross-stroke + a: read "uitia(?)"; alternatives "uiuunt a", "iuuanta". Its point of insertion (before or after iniquitatis) is uncertain. No run-over below line 24 visible in the images.
