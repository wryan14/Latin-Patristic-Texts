NOTES:
- 02/04: defitiat, fatiamus: the letter before i looks like t rather than c in both words (c/t close in this hand); alternatives deficiat, faciamus.
- 02: line end "quia" – the final a is at the gutter but visible.
- 04: "qd+bar cuq;" read quodcumque (same qd sign as quod in line 11); quecumque considered and rejected.
- 05: marginal lection mark in brown ink, "Lc" followed by a numeral, mottled: viii most likely, vii or iii possible. Line end "mali": final i in the gutter.
- 06: "huius" (hui + us-sign) added above ill. in a small, lighter later hand. Line end after uestigia: two or three letters in the gutter, read "ire" (possibly with e-caudata); not fully visible. A short horizontal stroke stands in the left margin between lines 6 and 7 (not text).
- 07: after "et" an erasure of roughly 5-6 letters (one word); faint traces (possibly "ut h.." or "uel ..", a descender in the middle) not legible. "istis": read iftif = istis; "ipsis" considered but no p seen.
- 08: main text "sustentat" with a bar over -at-; above the line "n" and a 2-shaped -ur sign have been added, giving sustentantur; whether the bar/n and -ur belong to the first hand or a corrector is unclear. "nostra" = nra with bar.
- 09: laudabiliter: "laudabilit" plus an -er sign disappearing in the gutter.
- 10: "preoptamus" (p with bar above + optam + us-sign) added above the line in a small, lighter later hand, with an insertion stroke after obtinere. The main-line word under/after it is blotted or cancelled: tall s + iu + a smeared ending; read siuis ("si uis") or possibly siue / suis. Treated as deleted because of the smear; it may simply be blotted and still valid.
- 13: iusticiam: a short bar above the final a, faint.
- 14, 16: final nasal bars at the gutter (uniuersum, commutationem) only partly visible.
- 16: after "patiatur" a 2-shaped -ur sign above and a paragraph/insertion flourish before "Aut".
- 17: "pro" written in the left margin/outdented, p with loop to the left.
- 18: "sortes": the final letters are ligatured (looks like "sorter"); with con- from line 17 = consortes.
- 19: abnegemus: "abnege" + abbreviation at the gutter, ending not clearly visible.
- 20: "ea" written in the left margin before "que" in lighter brown ink, apparently a later addition. Line end "aua": only "au" and part of a further letter visible.
- 21: an accent-like stroke over the a of "ritia". Line end "su-" (next line begins with per-abbreviation + bia = superbia).
- 22: line end "que" in the gutter, smudged; could be "que" with e-caudata or "qua".
- 23: line end "reg" + descending stroke/abbreviation in the stained gutter; read regnum (line 24 begins "dei"); possibly "reg-" only with "num" lost in the gutter.
- 24: "crucem": stained, final letters faint; a mark above may be a bar.
