NOTES:
01: "in ea" written without word space (inea); a small stroke stands above the a/d junction (accent or separation mark), not read as a letter.
03: both p's have the bar through the descender = per (permanet, peruenire). ualeamus with 9-sign.
04: "per opera": p with crossed descender = per; "quia" = q with stroke, twice in this passage (04, 06).
08: line ends short after "diligere." with a positura-like flourish at the binding; nothing further seen.
10: small triangular hole before "Omnes"; letters seen inside it ("ue"/"de"-like) belong to another leaf and are not transcribed. "Omnes" = Oms with bar; "autem" = aut with bar, last letters in the gutter.
11: "domino" = dno with bar, at the binding edge, legible.
14: "nostrorum" = nrorum-sign, legible at the edge.
16: a small round hole stands between "pigri." and "ad"; an "et"-like shape is visible inside/at it. I take it as writing of the other leaf showing through the hole; alternative: a real "et" of this page (pigri. et ad ista). "fideliter" = fidelit with bar at the edge.
17: a stroke above the e of "persoluenda" (accent), not an abbreviation.
19: "spu" with bar: spiritu or spiritum; ablative taken from context, hence (?).
21-23: three holes interrupt the lines; the scribe wrote around them (bo|nos, in|iustos); the strokes seen inside the holes are from another leaf.
22-24: at the left, beyond the curved edge, "pe…" and "di non a…" belong to the neighbouring leaf and are not transcribed.
24: last word cut by the torn lower right edge: "scir" followed by a trace (scire?); the rest is lost or on the missing parchment.
