04: "sustentator" with a small u written above the o (correction to sustentatur); recorded as deletion of o + added u, though the o is not visibly cancelled. Alternative: sustentator\u/.
06: the 9-sign of "timeamus" hangs low between lines 5 and 6 (under "Hunc mag"); it belongs to timeam-. Bar over "nificu" is faint/slanted but present.
07: "aeterni" written with ae-ligature (not e-caudata). "interi-": the first minims could be read "uiteri", taken as interi/tus.
10: "intolerabile" (no final s seen), then "frigus".
11: line begins beside a small marginal hole (strokes of another leaf show in it; ignored). After "nos" the page has only "a" before "celorum" (not "ad"); no noun (e.g. regna) after "felitia". 13: "uel" = ul with stroke through l. Small mark before the 2-sign of sentitur.
14: e of "timetur" is written raised above the line (tim^e tur), apparently because of the parchment defect just below; could be counted as a supralinear addition \e/. "dei" = di with bar, written close to "uisio" (alternative: diuisio, but the bar is there).
15: "quia" = q with superscript open-a sign; alternative "que"/"quam". Large hole after "cernitur" and a second smaller defect; writing seen in them belongs to another leaf.
16: "accuset" is split around the hole (ac | cuset). "nostri" = nri with bar.
17: between "no" and "s" of "nos" a small defect/mark (2-shaped stroke on the line) separates the letters; read simply nos. Alternative: something erased.
19: "egregii" written egregu (two minims + i); "ill." with stroke through the l's, nothing written above it.
20: "liter" = lit with bar. Punctus versus after "obtentu".
21: small stained defect above "senti" with a dark stroke in it; not read as text.
23: a point or pair of points stands inside "auxili:ante" (after auxili); ignored as a parchment mark/pen rest. "et" before "deo" is faint (ampersand, rubbed).
24: first word has a bar above and its first letter seems to have a descender (looks like "pro" with bar); read as nro = nostro from the shape of the abbreviation, doubtful.
